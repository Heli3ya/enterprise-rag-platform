Acme Corporation — People Operations
Policy: Remote and Hybrid Work
Policy owner: VP of People
Effective: 1 January 2026
Document code: HR-RW-003

1. PURPOSE

This policy explains who may work remotely, the expectations that apply to
remote work, and the equipment and allowances the company provides.

2. ELIGIBILITY

2.1 An employee becomes eligible for remote work after 6 months of
continuous employment.

2.2 The employee must have received a performance rating of "meets
expectations" or higher in the most recent review cycle.

2.3 Employees on a formal performance improvement plan are not eligible
until the plan is closed.

3. WORK MODELS

3.1 The default arrangement is hybrid: 3 days per week in the office and 2
days per week remote.

3.2 The specific in-office days are agreed with the line manager and must
include at least one day that overlaps with the rest of the team.

3.3 Fully remote work requires approval from both the Vice President of the
department and the department head.

3.4 Fully remote arrangements are reviewed every 12 months.

4. WORKING HOURS

4.1 Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local
time zone. Meetings should be scheduled inside this window whenever
possible.

4.2 Outside core hours, employees may arrange their schedule to suit their
personal circumstances, provided that weekly deliverables are met.

4.3 Employees must keep their calendar and their status indicator up to
date while working remotely.

5. EQUIPMENT AND ALLOWANCES

5.1 The company provides a laptop and a headset to every remote employee.

5.2 A one-time home office equipment allowance of 500 USD is available for
a desk, a chair and a monitor. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days.

5.3 An annual refresh allowance of 250 USD is available after the first
year.

5.4 A monthly internet allowance of 50 USD is paid with the regular salary.

5.5 Equipment purchased with the allowance remains company property and
must be returned when employment ends.

6. WORKSPACE REQUIREMENTS

6.1 The remote workspace must be private, lockable and free from
interruptions during core hours.

6.2 A home safety checklist must be completed before the first remote day.

6.3 Confidential conversations must not take place in public spaces.

7. SECURITY

7.1 Company data must only be accessed from a company-managed device.

7.2 The corporate VPN is mandatory when working from public or untrusted
networks.

7.3 Screens must be locked whenever the employee steps away from the
device.

8. CROSS-BORDER WORK

8.1 Employees may work from another country for a maximum of 20 business
days per calendar year.

8.2 Any cross-border work requires a tax and payroll review before travel,
which takes up to 15 business days.

8.3 Work from a country subject to international sanctions is not
permitted.

9. MEETING ETIQUETTE

9.1 Cameras must be switched on for external meetings and for customer
calls.

9.2 Cameras are optional for internal team meetings.

9.3 Meeting recordings require the consent of all participants.

10. PERFORMANCE AND REVIEW

10.1 Remote employees are assessed on outcomes and deliverables, not on
hours online.

10.2 Line managers hold a one-to-one meeting at least every 2 weeks with
each remote team member.

11. CONTACT
