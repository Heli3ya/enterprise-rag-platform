Acme Corporation — People Operations
Guide: New Employee Onboarding
Guide owner: People Operations Manager
Effective: 1 January 2026
Document code: HR-ON-001

1. BEFORE THE FIRST DAY

1.1 People Operations sends the welcome email 5 business days before the
start date. It contains the first-day schedule, the office address and the
name of the assigned buddy.

1.2 The hiring manager confirms the laptop specification at least 7 business
days before the start date so that IT can prepare the device.

1.3 The new employee completes the personal details form and the tax
declaration in the HR portal before the first day.

2. DAY ONE

2.1 The employee receives a badge, a laptop and a welcome kit from the
reception desk.

2.2 IT creates the email account, the directory account, the HR portal
account and the collaboration suite account within 4 hours of arrival.

2.3 The hiring manager holds a 30 minute welcome meeting and explains the
team charter.

2.4 The buddy takes the new employee to lunch and introduces the immediate
team.

3. FIRST WEEK

3.1 Security awareness training, which takes about 2 hours, must be
completed during the first week.

3.2 HR orientation covers payroll, benefits, the leave policy and the code
of conduct, and lasts 90 minutes.

3.3 The employee meets every direct team member in a 15 minute
introduction meeting.

3.4 The employee reads the department handbook and completes the
acknowledgement form.

4. BUDDY PROGRAM

4.1 Every new employee is assigned a buddy for the first 90 days.

4.2 The buddy is a peer from the same department, not the hiring manager.

4.3 The buddy holds a 30 minute check-in every week for the first month,
then every two weeks until day 90.

5. PROBATION

5.1 The standard probation period is 90 calendar days.

5.2 A formal review takes place at day 30, day 60 and day 90.

5.3 The probation may be extended once, by a maximum of 30 days, with
written notice at least 5 business days before the original end date.

5.4 Probation can be confirmed early after day 60 with manager and HR
agreement.

6. REQUIRED TRAINING

6.1 The following training must be completed within 14 calendar days of the
start date: security awareness, code of conduct, and anti-harassment.

6.2 Managers must additionally complete the "hiring and feedback" module
within 30 days.

6.3 Training completion is tracked in the learning portal and reported to
People Operations monthly.

7. GOALS AND FEEDBACK

7.1 Performance goals for the first six months are agreed with the manager
within 30 days of the start date.

7.2 The employee completes a short feedback survey on day 7, day 30 and day
90.

8. ACCESS AND TOOLS

8.1 Access requests are submitted through the IT portal. The service level
for a standard request is 2 business days.

8.2 Access to Confidential data requires the approval of the data owner and
is granted for a maximum of 12 months before review.

9. PAYROLL AND ADMINISTRATION

9.1 Salaries are paid monthly on the 25th. If the 25th falls on a weekend,
payment is made on the preceding business day.

9.2 Timesheets must be submitted by the 20th of each month.

9.3 The corporate expense card is issued after the probation period is
confirmed.

10. FIRST 90 DAYS CHECKLIST

10.1 Day 7: complete the onboarding survey and confirm that all accounts
work.

10.2 Day 30: first probation review and goal setting.

10.3 Day 60: second probation review and mid-point feedback.

10.4 Day 90: final probation review, confirmation letter and expense card
issuance.

11. CONTACT

Questions about onboarding should be sent to people@acme.example.
