Acme Corporation — Finance
Policy: Business Travel and Expense
Policy owner: Corporate Controller
Effective: 1 January 2026
Document code: FIN-TE-002

1. SCOPE

This policy covers all business travel, accommodation, meals and incidental
expenses incurred on behalf of Acme Corporation. It applies to employees,
contractors and guests travelling on company business.

2. APPROVAL

2.1 Travel must be approved before booking.

2.2 Trips with an estimated cost below 2,000 USD require line manager
approval. Trips between 2,000 USD and 5,000 USD require director approval.
Trips above 5,000 USD require Chief Financial Officer approval.

2.3 International travel additionally requires approval from the department
head, regardless of cost.

3. BOOKING

3.1 Flights and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel portal.

3.2 Bookings should be made at least 14 days before departure. Bookings
made with less than 7 days notice require a written justification.

3.3 Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium
economy may be booked for flights longer than 6 hours.

3.4 Business class is permitted only for flights longer than 9 hours and
requires Vice President approval.

4. ACCOMMODATION AND PER DIEM

4.1 The hotel limit is 150 USD per night for domestic travel.

4.2 For international travel the hotel limit is 250 USD per night in tier 1
cities and 180 USD per night in tier 2 cities.

4.3 The daily meal allowance is 45 USD for domestic travel and 80 USD for
international travel. In tier 1 international cities the allowance is 110
USD per day.

4.4 The per diem is paid as a flat allowance. Receipts are not required for
meals within the allowance, and unspent allowance is not reclaimed.

5. GROUND TRANSPORT AND MILEAGE

5.1 Public transport, ride sharing and airport transfers are reimbursable
with a receipt.

5.2 Employees who use a private vehicle for business travel are reimbursed
at 0.55 USD per mile.

5.3 Parking and tolls are reimbursable with a receipt.

6. RECEIPTS

6.1 Itemised receipts are required for every expense above 25 USD.

6.2 Receipts must show the date, the vendor, the amount and the currency.

6.3 Credit card statements are not accepted as a substitute for an itemised
receipt.

7. CORPORATE CARD

7.1 Flights, hotels and car rentals must be paid with the corporate card.

7.2 Meals and incidental expenses may be paid with a personal card and
reimbursed.

8. REIMBURSEMENT

8.1 Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip.

8.2 Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 business days.

8.3 Claims submitted more than 60 days after the trip end require Finance
Director approval.

9. NON-REIMBURSABLE ITEMS

9.1 Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable.

9.2 Personal entertainment, minibar charges, spa treatments and airline
lounge access purchased individually are not reimbursable.

9.3 Traffic fines and parking penalties are not reimbursable.

9.4 Airline upgrades purchased with personal funds are not reimbursable.

10. INSURANCE

10.1 Travel insurance is mandatory for every international trip and is
purchased by the company at the time of booking.

10.2 Domestic travel insurance is optional and is not reimbursed.

11. EXTENSIONS AND PERSONAL DAYS

11.1 Personal days added to a business trip are permitted but all costs for
those days are the responsibility of the employee.

11.2 The company does not reimburse accommodation or meals for personal
days.

12. CONTACT

Questions about this policy should be sent to travel@acme.example.
