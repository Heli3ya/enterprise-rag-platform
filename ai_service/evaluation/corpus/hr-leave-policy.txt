Acme Corporation — Human Resources
Policy: Annual, Sick and Parental Leave
Policy owner: HR Director
Effective: 1 January 2026
Document code: HR-LV-001

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the leave entitlements available to all full-time and
part-time employees of Acme Corporation and the process for requesting
leave. It applies to every department and to every employment contract
unless a local statutory rule is more generous.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE

2.1 Full-time employees accrue 26 working days of paid annual leave per
calendar year. Leave accrues at a rate of 2.17 days per completed month of
service.

2.2 Employees who join during the year receive a pro-rated entitlement
based on the number of complete months remaining in the calendar year.

2.3 Part-time employees receive the same entitlement pro-rated by their
contracted weekly hours.

2.4 A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the
following calendar year. Carried-over days must be used before 31 March;
after that date they expire and are not paid out.

2.5 Annual leave must be requested through the HR portal at least 5
working days before the first day of absence and requires line manager
approval. Requests for periods longer than 10 consecutive working days
also require HR Director approval.

3. SICK LEAVE

3.1 Employees receive full pay for the first 10 working days of certified
sickness absence in each calendar year.

3.2 Beyond the first 10 working days, sick leave is paid at 70 percent of
base salary for a maximum of 30 additional working days per calendar year.

3.3 A medical certificate issued by a licensed practitioner is required
for any sickness absence longer than 2 consecutive working days.

3.4 Employees must notify their line manager within 4 hours of the start
of their scheduled shift on the first day of sickness absence.

4. PARENTAL LEAVE

4.1 The primary caregiver is entitled to 90 calendar days of paid parental
leave. The leave may start up to 2 weeks before the expected delivery date.

4.2 The secondary caregiver is entitled to 30 calendar days of paid
parental leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the birth or
adoption.

4.3 Parental leave must be requested at least 30 calendar days in advance,
except in the case of a premature birth.

4.4 Parental leave does not interrupt annual leave accrual.

5. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE

5.1 Employees are entitled to 5 working days of paid bereavement leave for
the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent or sibling).

5.2 Employees are entitled to 2 working days of paid bereavement leave for
the death of an extended family member (grandparent, grandchild, aunt,
uncle, niece or nephew).

6. UNPAID LEAVE

6.1 Employees may request up to 60 calendar days of unpaid leave per
calendar year. Unpaid leave requires HR Director approval and cannot be
used to extend a notice period.

7. STUDY LEAVE

7.1 Every employee is entitled to 5 working days of paid study leave per
calendar year for examinations or professional certification.

7.2 Study leave must be requested at least 10 working days in advance and
supported by a registration document.

8. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS

8.1 The company observes 12 fixed public holidays and grants 4 additional
floating holidays that employees may take on dates of their choosing.

8.2 Floating holidays follow the same approval process as annual leave and
expire at the end of the calendar year.

9. EMERGENCY LEAVE

9.1 Where an emergency makes advance notice impossible, the employee must
inform their line manager within 4 hours of the start of the shift and
submit the request in the HR portal within 2 working days.

10. RECORD KEEPING

10.1 Leave balances are visible in the HR portal and are updated nightly.

10.2 Managers must approve or reject a leave request within 3 working days
of submission. A request that is not actioned within 3 working days is
escalated automatically to the department head.
