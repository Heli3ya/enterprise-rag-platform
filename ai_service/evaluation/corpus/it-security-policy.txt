Acme Corporation — Information Technology
Policy: Information Security
Policy owner: Chief Information Security Officer
Effective: 1 January 2026
Document code: IT-SEC-004

1. SCOPE

This policy applies to every employee, contractor and third party who
accesses Acme systems, data or networks, on any device and from any
location.

2. PASSWORDS AND AUTHENTICATION

2.1 Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not reuse any
of the previous 5 passwords.

2.2 Passwords must be rotated every 180 days. Rotating earlier is allowed
and does not count against the reuse limit.

2.3 Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every remote connection,
for the email system, for the HR portal and for all administrative
consoles. SMS is not accepted as a second factor; a hardware key or a
time-based authenticator application must be used.

2.4 An account is locked for 30 minutes after 5 consecutive failed login
attempts. Repeated lockouts within 24 hours are reported to the security
team.

2.5 Shared accounts are prohibited. Service accounts require a documented
owner and a rotation schedule approved by the security team.

3. DEVICES

3.1 Full-disk encryption is mandatory on every laptop and mobile device
that stores company data. BitLocker is required on Windows and FileVault on
macOS.

3.2 New devices must be enrolled in the mobile device management system
within 24 hours of being issued.

3.3 Devices must be configured to lock automatically after 5 minutes of
inactivity and must require authentication to resume.

3.4 Only company-issued, hardware-encrypted USB storage may be used.
Personal USB drives are blocked by endpoint protection.

4. NETWORK

4.1 The corporate VPN must be used whenever a company device connects from
a public or untrusted network.

4.2 Split tunnelling is disabled on all company devices. All traffic is
routed through the corporate gateway while the VPN is connected.

4.3 Guest Wi-Fi is isolated from the corporate network and must never be
used to access internal systems.

5. DATA CLASSIFICATION

5.1 Information is classified into four levels: Public, Internal,
Confidential and Restricted.

5.2 Confidential data must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Sharing
outside the company requires written approval from the data owner.

5.3 Restricted data may only be accessed from managed devices, only by
named individuals approved by the CISO, and must never be copied to
removable media.

5.4 Data must not be stored in personal cloud accounts. Approved storage is
the corporate document management system.

6. SOFTWARE

6.1 Software may only be installed from the approved software catalogue.

6.2 A request to add new software requires a security review, which is
completed within 5 business days.

6.3 Unapproved software found on a device is removed and the incident is
logged.

7. INCIDENT REPORTING

7.1 Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@acme.example
within 1 hour of detection.

7.2 A severity 1 incident, defined as confirmed data loss, ransomware or
compromise of an administrative account, triggers a page to the on-call
engineer, who must respond within 15 minutes.

7.3 Incidents must not be investigated by the reporting employee beyond
preserving evidence and disconnecting the affected device from the network.

8. ACCESS MANAGEMENT

8.1 Access reviews are performed quarterly by every department head.

8.2 Privileged and administrative access is reviewed monthly.

8.3 When an employee leaves the company, all accounts are disabled within
2 hours of the recorded departure time and deleted after 30 days.

8.4 Access requests are submitted through the IT portal and are fulfilled
within 2 business days.

9. LOGGING AND MONITORING

9.1 Authentication, authorisation and administrative action logs are
retained for 12 months.

9.2 Logs are shipped to the central logging platform within 5 minutes of
being generated.

10. SECURITY AWARENESS

10.1 Every employee completes security awareness training within 14 days of
joining and refreshes it annually.

10.2 Phishing simulation campaigns are run quarterly. An employee who
clicks more than twice in a rolling 12-month period must complete
additional training within 10 working days.

11. EXCEPTIONS

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the CISO and are
reviewed every 6 months.
