Acme Corporation — Procurement
Policy: Purchasing and Vendor Management
Policy owner: Head of Procurement
Effective: 1 January 2026
Document code: PROC-001

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy governs the purchase of goods and services on behalf of Acme
Corporation, including the selection of vendors, the approval of spend and
the payment of invoices. It applies to every department.

2. PURCHASE THRESHOLDS

2.1 Purchases below 1,000 USD require a single written quotation.

2.2 Purchases between 1,000 USD and 10,000 USD require at least three
written quotations from different vendors.

2.3 Purchases above 10,000 USD require a formal request for proposal (RFP)
process managed by Procurement.

2.4 Purchases above 50,000 USD additionally require a competitive
negotiation and a signed contract reviewed by Legal.

3. APPROVAL AUTHORITY

3.1 Purchases below 1,000 USD require line manager approval.

3.2 Purchases between 1,000 USD and 10,000 USD require director approval.

3.3 Purchases between 10,000 USD and 50,000 USD require Chief Financial
Officer approval.

3.4 Purchases above 50,000 USD require Chief Executive Officer approval.

3.5 No employee may approve a purchase that benefits a relative or a
company in which they hold a financial interest.

4. PURCHASE ORDERS

4.1 A purchase order is mandatory before any invoice is accepted.

4.2 Invoices without a matching purchase order are rejected by Finance.

4.3 The purchase order must reference the approved quotation or contract
number.

5. VENDOR ONBOARDING

5.1 Every new vendor must complete a security questionnaire and provide a
valid tax identification number.

5.2 Vendor onboarding takes up to 10 business days from the submission of
complete documentation.

5.3 Vendors handling Confidential or Restricted data must also complete an
annual security assessment.

5.4 The preferred vendor list is reviewed once per year by Procurement.

6. CONTRACTS

6.1 Any contract with a value above 25,000 USD or a duration longer than 12
months must be reviewed by Legal before signature.

6.2 Contracts must include a termination clause allowing cancellation with
30 days written notice.

6.3 Automatic renewal clauses longer than 12 months are not permitted.

7. EMERGENCY PURCHASES

7.1 An emergency purchase is permitted when a delay would cause a security
incident, a production outage or a legal breach.

7.2 Emergency purchases are capped at 5,000 USD.

7.3 Retrospective approval must be obtained within 2 business days, with a
written explanation of the urgency.

8. INVOICES AND PAYMENT

8.1 Standard payment terms are 30 days net from the invoice date.

8.2 A discount of 2 percent is available when an invoice is paid within 10
days.

8.3 Payments are made twice per month, on the 10th and the 25th.

9. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

9.1 Employees involved in procurement must declare any conflict of interest
annually.

9.2 Undeclared conflicts are treated as a disciplinary matter.

10. RECORD RETENTION

10.1 Quotations, purchase orders, contracts and invoices are retained for 7
years.

11. CONTACT
